About

I'm Bryan Jeanne Kennedy Castro, an SEO Specialist, Paid Ads Manager and WordPress Website Builder from Pampanga, Philippines. For over six years, I've helped businesses stop guessing and start ranking: finding exactly why a website isn't bringing in clients, then fixing it through audits, content, technical SEO and the WordPress build itself.

I've run end-to-end campaigns for clinics that needed to own their city's search results, schools competing for international enrolments, and finance platforms fighting for the country's most competitive keywords. Because I build WordPress sites myself, nothing gets lost between strategy and execution. The same person who spots the opportunity is the one who ships the fix.

I also plan and manage paid campaigns on Google Ads and Meta Ads, from budget and audience targeting to creative testing. And because I build the landing pages too, your ads never send traffic to a page that wasn't made to convert.

If your website looks good but doesn't bring you clients, that's not bad luck. It's a fixable problem. Send me your URL and I'll show you exactly what's holding it back.
